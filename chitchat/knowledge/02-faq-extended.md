# Extended FAQ › About the Company

## What does ESE-TECH do?
ESE-TECH Industrial Solutions Corporation is a Philippine engineering firm for industrial automation and power control. It designs, installs, programs and maintains control systems (PLC, HMI, SCADA), supplies and commissions drives (VFDs, low/medium voltage drives, MCCs), provides on-site field service, runs technical training, and performs energy efficiency audits.

## Is ESE-TECH the same company as ESETECH System Engineering?
Yes. ESE-TECH Industrial Solutions Corporation was formerly known as ESETECH System Engineering. It was DTI-registered on May 15, 2015 and incorporated with the SEC on March 29, 2017.

## How long has ESE-TECH been in business?
ESE-TECH has operated since its DTI registration on May 15, 2015, and became a corporation (SEC) on March 29, 2017.

## Who owns or leads ESE-TECH? Who is the boss?
ESE-TECH is led by its President and CEO, Elibienve Eltagon. The management team also includes Romar Manang (Sales and Business Development Manager), Jayson Labios (Automation Manager) and Roberto Baluyot (Project Manager).

## Who should I talk to about a purchase or quotation?
Sales and quotations are handled by the sales department, led by Sales and Business Development Manager Romar Manang. Email sales.dept@esetech.com.ph with your project requirements.

## Who handles automation or PLC projects?
Automation work falls under Automation Manager Jayson Labios. Start by emailing sales.dept@esetech.com.ph so the request is routed to the right engineer.

## Who manages projects?
Project execution is managed by Project Manager Roberto Baluyot.

# Extended FAQ › Coverage and Industries

## Do you serve clients outside Bataan? Do you cover Visayas and Mindanao?
Yes. ESE-TECH provides nationwide support across Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao. The head office is in Balanga City, Bataan.

## What industries do you work with?
Cement and aggregates, water and wastewater, power generation, food and beverage, and manufacturing.

## Do you work with cement plants?
Yes. Cement and aggregates is a core sector: drive systems and fan migrations for major facilities. ESE-TECH migrated two 2MW, 690V, 12-pulse low-voltage drives for a DOPOL fan at a major cement plant, which led to a follow-on 2.6MW drive system contract.

## Do you work with water utilities or wastewater plants?
Yes: control panels, high-performance pumps and VFD integration for water and wastewater.

## Do you work with food and beverage plants?
Yes: automation, sensor technology and drive systems.

## Do you work with power plants?
Yes: power distribution control and high-performance drive solutions for power generation.

## Are you a Danfoss partner?
Yes. ESE-TECH is an Authorized Value-Added Channel Partner and System Integrator of Danfoss Philippines for heavy industry.

Yes. As an official partner, ESE-TECH supports Rockwell Automation, Danfoss and ABB with engineering support, drives, PLCs and spare parts. Contact sales.dept@esetech.com.ph with the part number or the equipment model.

The ESE-TECH knowledge base lists Rockwell Automation, Danfoss and ABB as its official partner brands. For other brands, email sales.dept@esetech.com.ph with the brand and model to ask whether the team can help.

## How much does a VFD or PLC cost? What are your prices?
ESE-TECH does not publish prices; every quotation depends on the equipment rating, quantity and scope of work. Send your requirements to sales.dept@esetech.com.ph for a quote.

On-call emergency support is available for contract clients at 0949 940 1722. Other clients can email sales.dept@esetech.com.ph or call during office hours.

## Do you do site visits or on-site repairs?
Yes. On-site field services include high-response commissioning, preventive maintenance, emergency repair and site-specific troubleshooting.

## Do you offer preventive maintenance?
Yes. Preventive maintenance is part of ESE-TECH's on-site field services, and long-term maintenance is part of its system integration work.

## Do you offer training for our engineers or technicians?
Yes. ESE-TECH runs authorized, hands-on technical training programs on hardware and systems for client engineering teams.

## Can you help us lower our electricity bill?
Yes. ESE-TECH performs energy efficiency audits and motor/drive modernizations aimed at significantly reducing industrial power consumption. It is a Regular Corporate Member of the Philippine Energy Efficiency Alliance (PE2).

## Can you upgrade or replace old drives or control systems?
Yes. Modernization of motor and drive systems and the design and programming of new control systems are core services. The 2MW cement-plant fan drive migration is an example of a drive migration project.

## What is PE2? Is ESE-TECH a member of PE2?
PE2 is the Philippine Energy Efficiency Alliance, an organization advocating sustainable energy management. ESE-TECH is a Regular Corporate Member.
