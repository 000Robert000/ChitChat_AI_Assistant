# Service Request Guide › Technical Support Request

## What to prepare before requesting technical support
ESE-TECH asks clients to prepare three things before a support call or site assessment:
1. Location: the plant or site address.
2. Equipment brand and model, for example Rockwell (Allen-Bradley), Danfoss or ABB, including the model number from the nameplate.
3. A description of the issue.

Other details that usually speed things up:
- The exact fault or alarm code shown on the drive, PLC or HMI display
- When the problem started, and whether it is constant or intermittent
- What changed recently (new parameters, maintenance, power outage, new load)
- Photos of the drive nameplate, the fault screen and the panel
- Whether production is stopped (urgent) or still running

# Service Request Guide › Quotation Request

## What to include in a quotation request
Send your specific project requirements to sales.dept@esetech.com.ph. Helpful details:
- Type of work: supply only, supply and installation, programming, commissioning, retrofit/migration, maintenance contract, training or energy audit
- Equipment details: motor power (kW or HP), voltage, quantity, preferred brand (Rockwell, Danfoss or ABB)
- Application: pump, fan, conveyor, compressor, mixer, kiln, etc.
- Site location and target schedule
- Drawings, existing equipment lists or photos if available

# Service Request Guide › Emergencies

## What to do in an equipment emergency
1. Make the area safe first: stop the machine using the proper procedure and keep people away from energized equipment.
2. Note the fault code and take photos of the display if it is safe to do so.
3. Contract clients: call the on-call emergency line, 0949 940 1722.
4. Other clients: call or email sales.dept@esetech.com.ph during office hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
Only qualified, authorized personnel should open electrical panels or drives.
