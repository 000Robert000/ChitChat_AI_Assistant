# Glossary › Terms A to M

## Glossary: AC drive, affinity laws, commissioning, DC bus
- AC drive: another name for a Variable Frequency Drive (VFD).
- Affinity laws: rules for pumps and fans: flow follows speed, pressure follows speed squared, power follows speed cubed.
- Commissioning: testing and starting up newly installed equipment before handover.
- DC bus: the internal DC link inside a drive, stored in capacitors that stay charged after power-off.

## Glossary: DOL, DTI, harmonics, HMI, LOTO, LV, MCC, MV
- DOL (Direct-On-Line) starter: starts a motor at full voltage, with high inrush current; a VFD or soft starter avoids this.
- DTI: Department of Trade and Industry (Philippines). ESE-TECH was DTI-registered on May 15, 2015.
- Harmonics: distortion of the supply current caused by drives and other electronic loads; reduced by 12-pulse designs and filters.
- HMI: Human-Machine Interface, the operator screen.
- LOTO: Lockout/Tagout, the safety procedure for isolating energy before work.
- LV / MV: low voltage (up to about 690V AC for drives) / medium voltage (about 2.3kV and above).
- MCC: Motor Control Center, a cabinet line-up holding motor starters and drives.

# Glossary › Terms N to Z

## Glossary: PE2, PLC, PPE, retrofit, SCADA, SEC
- PE2: Philippine Energy Efficiency Alliance; ESE-TECH is a Regular Corporate Member.
- PLC: Programmable Logic Controller, an industrial controller that runs the machine logic.
- PPE: Personal Protective Equipment.
- Retrofit / migration: replacing old equipment, such as drives or controllers, with modern equipment while keeping the rest of the system.
- SCADA: Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition, plant-wide monitoring and control software.
- SEC: Securities and Exchange Commission (Philippines). ESE-TECH was incorporated on March 29, 2017.

## Glossary: soft starter, system integrator, VFD, value-added channel partner
- Soft starter: reduces motor starting current, but unlike a VFD it does not control running speed.
- System integrator: a company that designs and connects automation hardware and software into a working system.
- VFD: Variable Frequency Drive, which controls motor speed by varying frequency and voltage.
- Value-added channel partner: an authorized reseller that also provides engineering and support services; ESE-TECH holds this status with Rockwell Automation and Danfoss.
