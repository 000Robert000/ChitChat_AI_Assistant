# 1. Company Profile and Overview

ESE-TECH Industrial Solutions Corporation, formerly known as ESETECH System Engineering, is a premier engineering firm based in the Philippines. The company specializes in providing cutting-edge industrial automation and power control solutions tailored to heavy industry requirements.

- **Website:** www.esetech.com.ph
- **Head Office:** Landoz Building, Roman Superhighway, Cataning, Balanga City, Bataan, Philippines
- **DTI Registration:** May 15, 2015
- **SEC Incorporation:** March 29, 2017
- **Primary Industry:** Industrial Automation, Engineering Services, Power & Drive Systems

# 3. Core Business and Service Capabilities

ESE-TECH offers a comprehensive suite of services designed to optimize industrial operations through modern technology and expert engineering.

- **System Integration and Process Control:** Custom design, installation, programming, and long-term maintenance of industrial control systems including PLCs, HMIs, and SCADA architectures.
- **Power, Drives, and Instrumentation:** Specialized engineering for Variable Frequency Drives (VFD), low/medium voltage drives, motor control centers (MCC), and industrial sensors.
- **On-Site Field Services:** High-response commissioning, preventative maintenance, emergency repair, and site-specific troubleshooting.
- **Technical Training and Consultancy:** Authorized hands-on training programs to empower client engineering teams with hardware and system knowledge.
- **Energy Efficiency Audits:** Motor and drive system modernizations aimed at significantly reducing industrial power consumption and advocating sustainability.

# 4. Primary Industries and Geographical Coverage

ESE-TECH provides nationwide support across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao, serving critical sectors including:

- **Cement and Aggregates:** Drive systems and fan migrations for major facilities.
- **Water and Wastewater:** Control panels, high-performance pumps, and VFD integration.
- **Power Generation:** Power distribution control and high-performance drive solutions.
- **Food and Beverage:** Automation, sensor technology, and drive systems.
- **Manufacturing:** Custom automation and motor control for heavy industry.
